The day before the event, organizing committee chair Kotomin ([@kotomin_m](https://x.com/kotomin_m)) has published an **official complete guide** for attendees! It covers the whole flow of the day — venue access, check-in, the various activities, the social gathering, and even the unofficial event the following day.

## What's in the guide

- 📍 **Venue & check-in:** Sapporo Convention Center (about 10 minutes on foot from "Higashi-Sapporo Station" on the Tozai subway line). General check-in opens at 10:15 — please have your fortee QR code ready
- 🎤 **Sessions:** Opening at 10:40. 26 talks + 9 LTs (35 total) across 3 tracks. Online streaming available (the stream URL will be emailed during the day on 6/5)
- 🎉 **Activities:** 1-minute feedback, Ask the Speaker, sponsor booth stamp rally, lunch matching meetup, and more
- 🥩 **Social gathering:** 19:30–21:30 at "Genghis Khan Youyoutei Sapporo Main Branch" in Susukino. Same-day tickets will also be available
- 📅 **Unofficial event the next day:** A joint Vue / React / PHP / Laravel hands-on session and lunch meetup at the same venue

## For the day

Please also agree to the Code of Conduct, Privacy Policy, and photo/streaming/publication policy. If you're attending, please take a look ahead of time!
