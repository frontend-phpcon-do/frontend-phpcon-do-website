Hello! I'm Kotomin ([@kotomin_m](https://x.com/kotomin_m)), chair of the organizing committee for Frontend & PHP Conference Hokkaido 2026!

As we prepare for "Frontend & PHP Conference Hokkaido 2026," to be held on June 6, 2026, we're now accepting applications from companies who would like to sponsor the event.

For details about the conference, please see the note we published earlier.

## Event overview

- **Date:** Saturday, June 6, 2026 — 1 day + social gathering (offline)
- **Venue:** [Sapporo Convention Center](https://www.sora-scc.jp/)
- **Online:** Streaming planned
- **Official X:** [@fe_phpcon_do](https://x.com/fe_phpcon_do)

"Frontend & PHP Conference Hokkaido 2026" is a joint conference of two communities that have held past events: "[Frontend Conference Hokkaido](https://www.frontend-conf.jp/)" and "[PHP Conference Hokkaido](https://phpcon.hokkaido.jp/)."

## Application guidelines

Please refer to the page below for application guidelines and details.

**▶ [Sponsor application guidelines](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/10dZHSK51B8iN4OIMjpMRCY8AmGY1URyOETLQI4brjOc/edit?usp=sharing)**

[Sapporo Engineer Base](https://sapporo-engineer-base.dev/) has sponsored our venue costs, which has allowed us to significantly reduce the fixed costs of holding the conference.

Building on that support, we've set the sponsorship fees that sponsor companies bear as low as possible.

In recent years, transportation costs to Hokkaido and accommodation costs within Sapporo have risen sharply. When a sponsor's representative travels to the venue in Sapporo, this means business-trip and accommodation expenses arise on top of the sponsorship fee.

With this in mind, we wanted to do what we can to reduce situations where someone thinks, "I'd like to support the Hokkaido community, but the total cost makes it a high hurdle." So we've set the sponsorship fee itself as low as we could.

At the same time, over the past few years the overall cost of holding a technical conference — including venue, streaming, and operating costs — has been rising.

This time the fee is set lower than the general standard, but this is not intended to "lower the going rate for technical community conference sponsorships."

We simply ask for your understanding that it's the result of this event's particular circumstances.

We'd be delighted if this turns out to be a sponsorship program that companies who want to support the Hokkaido engineering community can take part in without strain.
