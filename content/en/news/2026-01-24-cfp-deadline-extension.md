Hello! I'm Kotomin ([@kotomin_m](https://x.com/kotomin_m)), chair of the organizing committee for Frontend & PHP Conference Hokkaido 2026!

We're currently accepting proposals for "Frontend & PHP Conference Hokkaido 2026," and we've decided to extend the submission deadline!

## About the deadline extension

**The deadline has been extended to 23:59 on Sunday, February 1, 2026!**

Submit here 👉👉 [https://fortee.jp/frontend-phpcon-do-2026/proposal/callfor](https://fortee.jp/frontend-phpcon-do-2026/proposal/callfor)

The reason for the extension is simple: we want to see even more proposals! We'd love to see all kinds of submissions — talks about PHP, talks about frontend, panel discussions, and more. To everyone who noticed at the last minute during the call for proposals: please, go ahead and submit 🙇‍♂️

That said, to everyone who submitted by January 25 (and those still finishing up): thank you so much! The whole organizing team is looking forward to seeing what kind of conference this becomes! 😆

Along with the deadline extension, there's an adjustment regarding selection. For talks submitted by the original deadline of Sunday, January 25, versus those submitted on or after Monday, January 26, we plan to take that timing into account slightly during selection. That said, this is not a promise of any explicit preferential treatment.

So everyone — please bring your hottest talks to Hokkaido! We're waiting for you!!

## Details about the call for proposals

Please check [this article (Call for Proposals is open)](/en/news/2025-12-28-cfp) for details about proposals!!

## We're looking for panel discussions!!

**This conference is accepting submissions for panel discussion (30-minute) slots!!**

> A panel discussion is a format where two or more panelists and one moderator talk through a back-and-forth exchange.
> For the moderator, you can decide in advance and apply with one, have an organizing staff member fill the role, or run it with panelists only and no moderator. Please indicate which format you intend in the talk summary or notes field so it's clear.

You can talk freely — discussing technology with coworkers or community friends, peppering someone you admire with questions, and more!

A talk with multiple people might be a little harder to apply for, but since it's a rare kind of session, we've set up a panel discussion slot betting on the chance that the best talks might be born from it! We'd be happy if submitting this proposal becomes the nudge that makes you think, "Maybe I'll reach out to that person."

We look forward to your submissions!!
