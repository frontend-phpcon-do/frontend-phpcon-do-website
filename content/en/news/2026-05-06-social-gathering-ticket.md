### What it's like

The social gathering is a place for attendees, speakers, sponsors, and staff to mingle.

If you're coming to the main event, I think joining the social gathering too will make for an even more enjoyable day! Let's have lots of exchange between frontend engineers and PHPers! 🙌

### Getting to the venue

After the main event of Frontend & PHP Conference Hokkaido 2026 ends, you'll head to the restaurant on your own. (I imagine attendees will probably make their way there together nicely.) You can't take part if you haven't completed social gathering check-in. Be sure to complete the separate social gathering check-in — distinct from the main event — during the main event hours. We'll provide details about the timing separately.

The restaurant sits in the heart of the city, 30 seconds on foot from Susukino Station, **diagonally across the intersection from the famous Black Nikka sign.** Dead center! 🩷 There are many hotels and izakaya nearby, making it easy to join for those coming from outside Hokkaido and easy to move on to an after-party. 🩵

From the main venue, Sapporo Convention Center, it takes about 22–25 minutes.

1. Sapporo Convention Center → Higashi-Sapporo Station on foot (about 12 min)
2. Higashi-Sapporo Station → Odori Station (🟠 Tozai Line, toward Miyanosawa), 3 stops (about 6 min)
3. Odori Station → Susukino Station (🟢 transfer to Namboku Line, toward Makomanai), 1 stop (about 1 min)
4. 30 seconds on foot to the right from Susukino Station Exit 2
