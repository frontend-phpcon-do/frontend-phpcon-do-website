During lunchtime on the day of the conference, we're running a "lunch matching" meetup (a.k.a. "Anti-Bocchi Lunch™")! Boxed lunches aren't provided at the venue, so we've put together a plan for attendees to enjoy lunch together.

## Overview

- Meet-up time: **around 12:30–12:50** (timed so that those who join Ask the Speaker can still make it)
- Meet-up location and other details will be shared on the official X account and during the opening on the day
- A flier with a lunch map will be handed out on the day

The afternoon sessions start at 14:10, so please head back with time to spare.

## Who it's for

This being a joint Frontend + PHP conference, it's a chance to meet attendees from regions, roles, and experience levels you don't usually run into. Beyond listening to talks, sharing your impressions and what you learned with other attendees is one of the most important parts of the conference experience.
