## Introduction

At Frontend & PHP Conference Hokkaido 2026, we're running a **travel support program for students and U25 attendees living in Hokkaido, covering transportation to the venue in Sapporo and accommodation for the day before and the day of the event.**

The first half of this note introduces the support program and how to apply; the second half is about my personal feelings on it.

If you're not eligible this time, I'd be grateful if you could share it with acquaintances and friends!

## Event overview

### What is a technical conference?

Some of you reading this note may not be very familiar with **"technical conferences."** This technical conference is **an event led by a tech community** — a gathering of engineers who work at various companies. Normally they work as engineers, and they get together on weeknights and weekends to give technical talks and to socialize.

Even within tech communities, a "conference" like this one is a large-scale event. **Engineers gather not only from Sapporo, where the venue is, but from various regions of Hokkaido and all across Japan!**

### What kind of event is Frontend & PHP Conference Hokkaido 2026?

This is the first joint event combining the previously held "Frontend Conference Hokkaido" and "PHP Conference Hokkaido." Frontend engineers and PHP engineers will of course attend — and so will many people studying other technical fields!

- **Date:** Saturday, June 6, 2026 — 1 day + social gathering (offline)
- **Venue:** Sapporo Convention Center
- **Official X:** [@fe_phpcon_do](https://x.com/fe_phpcon_do)
- **Official site:** [https://frontend-php-con.hokkaido.jp/](https://frontend-php-con.hokkaido.jp/)

Even if you've never used frontend or PHP, or never touched web technology at all, anyone with interest is welcome to attend! Truly, anyone!

By attending an event in a technical field you've never worked in, I hope you'll find new knowledge and new sparks.

## Support program details

### Support amounts

This time we'll support travel costs of up to **40,000 yen for students and up to 20,000 yen for U25 attendees.**

**[Students]**

- Transportation: up to 20,000 yen round trip
- Accommodation: up to 20,000 yen total (only nights of 6/5 and 6/6 are eligible, two nights combined)
- **Total possible support: 40,000 yen**

**[U25]**

- Transportation: up to 10,000 yen round trip
- Accommodation: up to 10,000 yen total (only nights of 6/5 and 6/6 are eligible, two nights combined)
- **Total possible support: 20,000 yen**

※ We'd like to be as flexible as possible with travel support. If you're planning to stay with friends or have a similar situation, please note it in the consultation field of the form. If you're even a little curious, please reach out first!

### Application eligibility

- Students: must be a student in high school or above (high school, technical college, vocational school, university, graduate school, etc.)
  - If you are a minor (under 18), you must have your guardian's consent
- U25: must be a working adult aged 25 or under
- Must have your residence based in Hokkaido (a dormitory or lodging in Hokkaido is also OK)
- Must be able to agree to the various terms (※ details in the form)
- Must be able to join the Discord server and publish a blog post afterward (※ details in the form)

## My feelings about the travel support program

### Why we do it

**Because using a travel support program to attend a conference changed my life.**

I'm from a small town called Hidaka in Saru District, Hokkaido. It's over two hours one way to Sapporo and costs nearly 5,000 yen round trip, so as a student it wasn't an easy place to casually travel to Sapporo from.

Thinking **"if travel support means I can get to Sapporo,"** I happened to attend a conference with a friend. There I witnessed a new world — "so these kinds of people exist," "so this kind of industry exists" — and I still remember the feeling of my world changing.

If conferences weren't being held in Hokkaido, I would not have become the committee chair. There's no doubt that **"I could attend precisely because it was held nearby."** That's why we hold it in Hokkaido, and why I want to provide travel support if there's any chance it could change even one person's life.

### Why I want students and young people to come

**Because they're bundles of potential.** Just being young means having so much potential. It's true.

By starting to attend events like this in my teens and twenties, I was able to keep expanding my own potential. Back in my student days, when I hadn't even considered whether to become an engineer, I attended a conference, and I became an engineer simply because the engineers I happened to talk to looked like they were having fun.

At the time, I wasn't going to the conference intending to change my life. I just **got casually invited, it looked fun so I went, and then it was fun.** That's all it needs to be.

I believe life becomes more enjoyable and richer by meeting many people. By visiting places you don't usually go, I hope you'll meet all kinds of people, talk, learn, and experience that kind of fun day!

### Please apply

- Because you're free on 6/6.
- Because you're interested in programming and making things.
- Because you want to make friends.
- Because you can get to Sapporo with your costs covered.

Any reason is fine! Even if you've never used frontend or PHP technologies, if you're interested, I really want you to go ahead and apply!

**To you, living in Hokkaido — I want you to come. I'm waiting for you!**
